# Atlantis City Shield

Protect Atlantis or another build with a powered, surveyed energy shield for **Stargate Journey**. The controller displays the shield and its power status; a separate emitter generates the field.

This guide covers the 1.0.4 Forge and NeoForge source releases. Settings and behavior can differ in older builds or modpacks.

## What the addon includes

- A shield controller, separate emitter, and survey tool.
- A configurable city-scale energy field with a holographic preview and live size control.
- ZPM and ordinary FE power options with a controller power monitor.
- A matter barrier, impact effects, and local weather protection.

{: .warning }
The formed shield can damage or kill players trying to cross it, including creative players with default settings. Read [Protection]({{ '/guides/protection.html' | relative_url }}) before activating it.

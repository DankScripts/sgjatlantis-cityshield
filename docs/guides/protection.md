# Protection and crossing

The formed surface intercepts projectiles, lightning and attacks, limits explosion effects across the boundary, and blocks piston movement through it. It also suppresses natural and chunk-generation mob spawning inside the fully formed shield; existing mobs and spawner-created mobs are not removed.

The shield forms progressively, so unformed areas are not yet protected. Test compatibility with other mods before relying on it against every kind of damage or effect. Rain and other weather visuals are suppressed under the active shield.

{: .warning }
There is no general friendly-player pass-through in this release. Contact with the formed boundary normally causes major damage and repels survivors. Creative bypass is **off by default**; enable it in the common config if desired. The planned frequency-tuned personal shield is not an available bypass in these releases.

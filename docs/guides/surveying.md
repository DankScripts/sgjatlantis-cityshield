# Surveying

{: .warning }
Turn the shield off before surveying. Its surface can damage or kill players trying to cross it, including creative players with the default settings.

1. Hold the Shield Surveyor and sneak-use the controller to bind the tool.
2. Sneak-use the air to cycle the selected mark; use a block to record that mark.
3. Mark **CENTER, NORTH, SOUTH, EAST, WEST, TOP, and BOTTOM**. Center is the middle of the area at water level, not a point high above the city.
4. North and south share one radius, as do east and west. Mark the farther edge in each pair last. **BOUNDARY** is an optional additional horizontal edge for expanding the shape.
5. Use the air without sneaking to open settings. Check the preview and select **Commit**. Adjust the settings if needed, or use **Cancel** to restore the committed geometry.

The settings screen also has a **10–100%** size slider. It scales horizontal radii around the surveyed center while preserving the upper and lower heights. The slider can be used while the shield is active; return it to 100% to restore the surveyed width.

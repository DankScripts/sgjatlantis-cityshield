# Troubleshooting

## No emitter found

Confirm the emitter is in the same dimension, within range, and not already linked to another controller. Add unprogrammed communication crystals to extend range.

## No power or shield drops

Check the charged hub and cable path, or supply enough ordinary FE through enough emitter faces. Check the actual common config and the controller's live monitor. A redstone signal cannot replace a power source.

## Preview does not cover the city

Turn the shield off, check that CENTER is at water level, re-mark the farther edges last, then preview and commit. Check the size slider is at 100%.

## Controls unavailable

Check operator permissions and whether redstone mode is enabled. The Surveyor must be bound to the controller and used in the same dimension.

## Players take damage crossing

This is the default matter-barrier behavior. Lower or deactivate the shield before crossing, or have an administrator enable creative bypass for creative players.

When reporting a problem, include the City Shield, Stargate Journey, Minecraft and loader versions, steps to reproduce, and the relevant server log.

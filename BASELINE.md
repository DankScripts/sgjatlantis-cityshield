## Historical baseline: 1.0.1-beta.2

The previous Forge 1.20.1 `1.0.1-beta.2` release baseline was locked on
2026-09-03. The historical JAR and preservation contract are local archival references;
they are not required for a source build or included in the source ZIP.

The baseline includes full client lighting on only the four powered rear
lightbar faces:

- block light `15`;
- sky light `15`;
- ambient occlusion disabled.

The standby model still selects `atlantis_prop_lightbar_off`. Shield mechanics,
power use, controller behavior, and all other resources remain unchanged.
